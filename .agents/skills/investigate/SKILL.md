# Investigate

Use the [shared discovery workspace contract](../../../docs/supporting-skills.md)
to load the workspace and save evidence, gaps, and contradictions.

1. Identify the questions whose answers could change a decision, reduce a
   material risk, or establish feasibility. Reuse existing evidence and open
   questions before collecting more.
2. Gather sources with direct knowledge of the question. Check authority,
   recency, applicability, and corroboration. Record each material observation
   with a source locator and any relevant date or version. A stakeholder
   statement establishes what was said. Verify the underlying claim separately
   when it matters.
3. Assess which claims are verified, which remain assumptions, and what limits
   confidence in each. Compare feasible alternatives against confirmed outcomes
   and constraints when the question warrants it. Identify missing tests or
   evidence that could change the conclusion.
4. Save supported observations, material assumptions, evidence gaps, and
   contradictions. In v3, save findings with evidence IDs, an assessment, and
   its rationale. Link them to affected decisions and render from saved state.
   In v1 and v2, link findings to ledger IDs in `investigation.md`. Explain
   what would close each gap. Leave conflict resolution to the stakeholder.
