# Model product

Load the [shared discovery workspace](../../../docs/supporting-skills.md) and
identify the decision, dependency, or ambiguity a model could clarify. If a
model adds no value, explain why and skip creating a model.

1. Choose a representation suited to the product and question. A space
   adjacency table can expose physical layout constraints. A responsibility
   map can clarify authority in an organization. A workflow can reveal
   handoffs in a service. Use the simplest useful form.
2. Show the relevant elements and relationships at discovery level. Tie them
   to evidence and decision IDs, mark assumptions and unresolved relationships,
   and show competing interpretations when sources conflict. Leave detailed
   implementation, dimensions, or specifications to later design unless they
   are already confirmed discovery constraints.
3. Save newly identified gaps and contradictions in the ledger. In `model.md`,
   record the model's purpose, the representation and why it fits, the model
   itself, and the questions or decisions it exposes. If skipping on a rerun,
   explain whether the existing model still applies to the current revision.
